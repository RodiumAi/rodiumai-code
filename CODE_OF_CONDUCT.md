# Code of conduct

This space is for people who use the RodiumAi developer products and for the people who build them. Help us keep it useful and welcoming.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not with the person.
- Stay on the subject of the issue you are in.
- Give enough detail for others to understand and to help.
- Accept that a maintainer may edit, lock or close an issue to keep the tracker readable.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Spam, advertising and content unrelated to the products.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Publishing secrets, such as API keys or passwords, yours or anybody else's.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can remove content and block people who do not follow these rules. To report a behaviour problem, use the [contact page](https://www.rodiumai.io/help/contact).
